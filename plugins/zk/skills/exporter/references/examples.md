# The Importance of Trigram Indexing

Trigram indexing is a powerful technique for fuzzy search. It builds upon the concept of **N-grams** (a contiguous sequence of *n* items from a given sample of text or speech) to provide efficient substring matching.

In our implementation, we use this to speed up searches across the note vault.
